Each miner has its own schedule. It switches between that miner's presets (the High / Middle / Low / Lowest power the tuner built, or your own from Profiles). Each change puts on the preset's clock, voltage, PV and fan curve once; a preset you apply by hand in between stays on until the next scheduled change. Times are the app's local time (—).

This week

each block = 30 minutes

Pick a preset, then click a block to paint it, or hold and drag to paint several (drag down to cover several days). The blocks you don't paint follow The rest of the time under the brushes: nothing (after a painted stretch the miner goes back to what it ran before), or a preset (shown lighter). ↻ Restart marks a restart at the start of a block (click a mark again, or use ⌫ Erase, to remove it). Each block is half an hour. Nothing changes on the miner until you press Save schedule.

On a phone each day takes two lines: 00:00–12:00 on the first, 12:00–24:00 on the second.

Restarts

off

Restart this miner's mining software on a schedule (the same as its Restart button: hashing stops for a minute or two). Mark restarts on the week above with ↻ Restart, pick a repeat here, or both. It's separate from the preset changes and works even without presets. A restart that comes due while the miner is being tuned is skipped. Every restart shows as ↻ (a ringed block) on the week.
