What this app does

Blake2b ASIC Control changes settings on your mining hardware: clock speed (MHz), voltage (mV), PV and fan speed. The tuner, presets and fan curves change them automatically. Settings other than the manufacturer's defaults can:

  • damage hash chips, hash boards, power supplies and fans, or shorten their life
  • make the hardware overheat and, in the worst case, cause a fire
  • void your warranty
  • lower or stop your hashrate and earnings

Automatic doesn't mean safe

The tuner's tests, the presets it builds and the fan curves can be wrong, and a setting that passed a test can still fail later (a hotter room, an aging chip, a dusty fan). The safety limits lower the risk; they can't remove it. Keep your miners somewhere heat, smoke or fire would be noticed, and check on them.
